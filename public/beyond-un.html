Beyond UN

A people-centric, experimental global governance initiative exploring legitimacy, ethics and cooperation beyond state-centric power structures.

Vision & Mission

A future of global governance where legitimacy is derived from ethical authority, inclusive participation and collective responsibility, not power or privilege.

Incubated by ACRI, Beyond UN operates as an independent, non-state platform for research, deliberation and norm creation.

The Problem

  • Decision-making dominated by veto power
  • Legitimacy constrained by state politics
  • Slow responses to global crises
  • Institutions rooted in a mid-20th-century world order

The Idea

Beyond UN does not replace the United Nations.

It builds what the UN cannot.

What Beyond UN Is

  • A platform for post-state global governance thinking
  • A space for norm creation before power
  • A bridge between ideas, ethics and action
  • A legitimacy-building initiative beyond formal treaties

What Beyond UN Is Not

  • Not anti-UN
  • Not a world government
  • Not a Model UN simulation
  • Not a state-led forum

How We Work

  • Independent research and policy labs
  • Global fellowships and expert cohorts
  • Parallel assemblies and deliberative forums
  • Public frameworks and non-binding outputs

ACRI's Role

ACRI provides institutional stewardship, ethical oversight and long-term continuity to Beyond UN, while safeguarding intellectual independence and openness to global participation.
