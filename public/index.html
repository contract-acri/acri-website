About the Centre

An institution built for the long horizon.

Two years of building research culture in Ahmedabad.Est. 2024 · Celebrating our 2nd anniversary, 2024–2026

The Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation (ACRI) was established to strengthen research culture, advance knowledge, and contribute meaningfully to academic discourse and public understanding.

ACRI is non-political, non-religious and non-partisan. It favours depth over speed, measured growth over rapid expansion, and institutional continuity over individual presence, so that its work remains credible for generations.
