Governance

Overview

ACRI follows a governance framework designed to ensure institutional clarity, independence and long-term continuity.

Governance Philosophy

  • Clear accountability and executive responsibility
  • Independence from political, religious and partisan influence
  • Separation between executive governance and academic activity
  • Institutional continuity over individual presence
  • Restraint in formalisation and expansion

Executive Leadership

ACRI follows a president-led executive structure. The President serves as the executive head of the institution and holds responsibility for institutional direction, continuity, appointments, representation and safeguarding independence. The President is supported by a Vice President and a Provost. See the institutional structure.

Oversight

Institutional oversight bodies provide custodial support in exceptional circumstances, without engaging in day-to-day management or academic decision-making. Academic matters are considered by the Academic Council.

Executive & Programmatic Roles

Executive and programmatic roles are appointed based on institutional need, with defined scopes, fixed terms and service-oriented responsibilities.

Independence & Conflict of Interest

A disclosure-based approach governs conflicts of interest, requiring transparency, integrity and recusal where appropriate.

Records & Continuity

Internal records are maintained to support continuity, accountability and legal compliance.

Legal Status

ACRI is pursuing formal incorporation as a not-for-profit entity, in compliance with applicable statutory requirements. Updates will be published on this website.
