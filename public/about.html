About Us

The Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation (ACRI) is an independent research and innovation institution based in Ahmedabad, India.

ACRI exists to strengthen research culture, advance knowledge, and contribute meaningfully to academic discourse and public understanding through integrity, rigour and intellectual openness. Its work spans research programmes, Schools of Thought, scholarly journals, student initiatives and public dialogue.

Vision

Our vision

To build a credible, independent and enduring research and innovation institution that advances knowledge through integrity, rigour and intellectual openness, and contributes meaningfully to academic discourse and public understanding.

Mission

  • Support high-quality, ethical and interdisciplinary research
  • Provide reliable platforms for academic publishing and scholarly exchange
  • Encourage evidence-based inquiry with institutional neutrality
  • Build research capacity among students and early-career scholars
  • Foster collaboration without ideological alignment

Institutional Philosophy

ACRI operates as a non-political, non-religious and non-partisan institution. It is not a degree-granting university: its Schools of Thought, Academy and student programmes complement formal education rather than replace it.

ACRI prioritises depth, quality and long-term institutional credibility over rapid expansion.

Governance & Independence

ACRI follows a president-led executive structure designed to ensure clarity of responsibility and long-term continuity. Institutional roles reflect a commitment to academic service and institutional responsibility rather than personal or commercial gain.
