Office of External Affairs

The central institutional authority for ACRI's external relations, international engagement and official communications.

Mandate and Institutional Role

The Office of External Affairs (OEA) coordinates, oversees and regulates ACRI's external interactions.

This includes the issuance of official statements, notices and institutional communications on matters of international relevance. The Office is the primary interface between ACRI and governments, multilateral institutions, academic bodies, journals and other external stakeholders, safeguarding the integrity, consistency and authenticity of ACRI's external positions.

External Engagement Scope

  • Governments and public authorities
  • Multilateral institutions
  • Academic and research bodies
  • Scholarly journals and publishers
  • International organisations
  • External institutional stakeholders

Governance and Oversight

OEA operates under the authority of the President of ACRI within clearly defined institutional protocols. External engagement undertaken by ACRI offices, centres or affiliates is expected to conform to OEA guidelines to ensure clarity of representation and institutional coherence.

Official Communications

All official external communications of ACRI, including institutional statements, formal notices and clarifications, are issued or routed through established and verifiable ACRI platforms, with accuracy, restraint, attribution and alignment with international law and multilateral frameworks.

Principles Guiding External Engagement

  • Respect for international law and multilateral frameworks
  • Institutional neutrality and non-alignment
  • Peaceful dialogue and responsible exchange of knowledge
  • Accuracy, restraint and accountability in communication
  • Clear attribution and procedural integrity

Verification and Reference

This website is the official reference point for verifying ACRI's external mandate, communication protocols and institutional authority.
