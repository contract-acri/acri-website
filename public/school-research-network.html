School Research Network

A structured institutional network through which schools engage with ACRI's research ecosystem, enabling early exposure to research thinking, documentation and inquiry, without instruction, evaluation or performance pressure.

On this page

  • How the Network Operates
  • What the Network Enables

How the Network Operates

Participating School

Identifies interested students and appoints a School Research Coordinator to liaise with ACRI.

Office of Research Secretariats

Coordinates student research engagement, documentation, reporting and procedural support.

Director of Students

Oversees student safeguarding, welfare, ethical compliance and conduct during research engagement.

Director of Research

Provides academic oversight, ensures rigour and aligns student involvement with approved ACRI programmes.

What the Network Enables

Student research engagement takes place strictly within defined ACRI research programmes. Students do not undertake independent projects in isolation; they participate in observation, documentation and guided inquiry linked to ongoing institutional research.

The default programme for school-level engagement is Beyond 2030, run by the Office for the Post-2030 Development Research Programme. Alternative themes may be proposed formally and are subject to internal review and approval.

What This Is

  • Early exposure to research culture
  • Structured, governed student engagement
  • Documentation and inquiry-based participation
  • Institutionally supervised involvement

What This Is Not

  • No competitions or rankings
  • No certificates or credentials
  • No testing or performance assessment
  • No independent unsupervised projects

Joining the Network

Participation is based on institutional alignment, seriousness of engagement and adherence to ACRI's research governance framework. Schools apply online; the Office of Research Secretariats reviews every application and writes to the School Research Coordinator.

Apply to join the networkView member schools
The ACRI School Research Network is a non-degree, non-certification, non-evaluative institutional initiative. Participation does not constitute academic credit, training or formal research qualification. Questions: office.secretariats@acriindia.org
