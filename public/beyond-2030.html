Flagship Research Programme

Beyond 2030

Establishing the foundation for the next global development agenda through slow, rigorous and archival research. Patient · Descriptive · Conceptual · Long-horizon.

On this page

  • Core Question
  • Institutional Positioning
  • A Four-Year Programme by Design
  • What This Programme Is Not
  • Participation by Schools

Core Question

If the Sustainable Development Goals formally end in 2030, what kinds of development frameworks might follow, and on what evidence should they be built?

Institutional Positioning

Beyond 2030 is ACRI's inaugural flagship research programme, designed to establish the Centre as a long-horizon institution committed to rigorous documentation, conceptual clarity and intellectual continuity beyond policy cycles.

A Four-Year Programme by Design

2026 · Current

Documentation

Country-wise archival record of official SDG statements.

2027

Aggregation

Global and regional pattern identification.

2028

Gap Analysis

Conceptual gaps and emerging issues.

2029

Post-2030 Thinking

Speculative frameworks beyond the SDGs.

What This Programme Is Not

  • Not SDG performance monitoring
  • Not ranking or scoring countries
  • Not advocacy or activism
  • Not implementation or policy advice

Participation by Schools

Beyond 2030 is the default programme for school-level engagement under the ACRI School Research Network.
