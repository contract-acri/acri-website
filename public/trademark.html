Ownership

The names Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation™ and ACRI™ are proprietary identifiers associated with the activities, publications, programmes, services, initiatives and digital platforms of Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation.

Unless otherwise stated, all trademark rights and interests in the names “Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation” and “ACRI” are claimed by Shah Fenil Pritesh.

Trademark Status

Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation™ (ACRI™) is claimed as a trademark. The use of the ™ symbol indicates a claim of trademark rights and does not imply that the mark is registered.

Any trademark application, registration or related status shall be determined solely by reference to the records of the relevant trademark authority.

Permitted Use

References to Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation (ACRI) for academic, educational, journalistic, informational, scholarly or descriptive purposes are permitted, provided such use:

  • Does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, accreditation or affiliation where none exists;
  • Does not create a likelihood of confusion regarding identity or authority;
  • Does not misrepresent any relationship with ACRI or its officers, centres, schools, programmes or initiatives.

Restricted Use

Without prior written authorisation, no individual or organisation may:

  • Represent itself as ACRI or as an official unit, centre, school, office, programme, project or affiliate of ACRI;
  • Use the ACRI name, acronym, branding, insignia, logos or identifiers in a manner likely to cause confusion;
  • Use ACRI marks in connection with misleading, deceptive, competing or unauthorised activities;
  • Register or operate domain names, social media accounts, publications, organisations or business identifiers that falsely suggest an association with ACRI.

Copyright

Unless otherwise stated, original content published on this website is protected by applicable copyright laws. Copyright in specific works may belong to Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation, individual authors, contributors, licensors or other rights holders as indicated.

Contact

For enquiries concerning trademarks, branding, licensing, copyright, intellectual property or authorised use of ACRI identifiers, please contact office.president@acriindia.org.
