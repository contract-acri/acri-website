What is ACRI Thinkers Lab?

A student-oriented intellectual forum that cultivates habits of reading, discussion, argumentation and reflective thinking beyond formal instruction.

The Lab does not function as a teaching programme and does not promote ideological positions or advocacy. The emphasis is on disciplined conversation grounded in evidence, clarity of thought and openness to differing viewpoints.

Core orientation

Dialogue before debate · Inquiry before opinion · Reason before rhetoric · Openness before alignment

Objectives

  • Encourage independent and critical thinking among students
  • Foster a culture of reading, dialogue and inquiry
  • Develop skills of articulation, listening and reasoned disagreement
  • Provide a neutral space for engagement with ideas and public issues
  • Support intellectual maturity and civic responsibility

Activities & Formats

  • Reading circles and text-based discussions
  • Guided group dialogues
  • Structured debates and issue discussions
  • Guest conversations with scholars and practitioners
  • Writing, reflection and idea-exchange sessions
Activities are discussion-based and exploratory. They do not lead to certification, academic credit or formal assessment.

Who Can Participate?

  • Open to students associated with ACRI initiatives
  • Participation based on interest and seriousness of engagement
  • No disciplinary or ideological restrictions
  • Participants maintain respectful conduct and intellectual openness

Institutional Context

ACRI Thinkers Lab operates under the Office of the Director of Students and aligns with ACRI's philosophy of integrity, rigour and intellectual openness. Contact: director.students@acriindia.org
